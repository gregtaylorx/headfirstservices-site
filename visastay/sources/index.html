Official sources

Where VisaStay's visa, tax-residency, and pre-arrival data comes from — last reviewed September 2026.

VisaStay is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government or immigration authority. Visa and immigration rules can change without notice. Always confirm your specific situation on the official government source(s) linked below, or with a licensed immigration professional, before making travel decisions.

For major, high-traffic destinations, every rule is verified directly against the destination's official government immigration authority. For the remaining destinations, rules are cross-checked against multiple independent travel-data services rather than a single source, and updated whenever those services or a user's "Report change" flag a discrepancy.

Tax residency rules often involve more than a simple day count (ties, domicile, split-year and lookback provisions). VisaStay's in-app alerts track the day-count trigger only — always confirm with a licensed tax professional before making a residency decision.
