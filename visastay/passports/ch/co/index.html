Do Swiss citizens need a visa for Colombia?

No. Swiss citizens can enter Colombia visa-free for up to 90 days. The stay can be extended by up to 90 days.

EntryNo visa needed
Maximum stay90 days
ExtensionYes, up to 90 days
Multiple entriesAllowed
