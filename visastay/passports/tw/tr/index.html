Do Taiwanese passport holders need a visa for Turkey?

Yes. Taiwanese passport holders need an eVisa, applied for online before travelling to Turkey. It allows stays for up to 30 days. An extension is not confirmed, so plan to leave within the permitted stay.

Maximum stay30 days
