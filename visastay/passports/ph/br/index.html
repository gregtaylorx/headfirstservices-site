Do Filipino citizens need a visa for Brazil?

No. Filipino citizens can enter Brazil visa-free for up to 90 days. The stay can be extended by up to 90 days.

EntryNo visa needed
Maximum stay90 days
ExtensionYes, up to 90 days

Visa-free, up to 90 days; extendable by 90 days

Official information: https://www.gov.br/mre/en/consular-services/visas
