Do German citizens need a visa for Vietnam?

No. German citizens can enter Vietnam visa-free for up to 45 days. An extension is not confirmed, so plan to leave within the permitted stay.

EntryNo visa needed
Maximum stay45 days
ExtensionNot confirmed

45-day visa exemption runs through 14 Aug 2028; 90-day e-Visa also available as alternative

Official information: https://evisa.gov.vn/
