Do Norwegian citizens need a visa for Indonesia?

Norwegian citizens can get a visa on arrival in Indonesia, valid for up to 30 days. The stay can be extended by up to 30 days.

EntryVisa on arrival
Maximum stay30 days
ExtensionYes, up to 30 days
Multiple entriesAllowed

Visa on arrival / e-VOA (evisa.imigrasi.go.id), 30 days

Official information: https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/
