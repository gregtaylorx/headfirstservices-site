Do New Zealand citizens need a visa for Mexico?

No. New Zealand citizens can enter Mexico visa-free for up to 180 days. An extension is not confirmed, so plan to leave within the permitted stay.

EntryNo visa needed
Maximum stay180 days
ExtensionNot confirmed
Multiple entriesAllowed
