VisaStay Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HeadFirstServices LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles your information in VisaStay — a visa day and tax residency tracker for frequent travelers. It covers VisaStay only; each of our apps has its own policy and its own separate database.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account you provide your email address and a password (stored as a salted hash — we never see it in plain text), or you sign in with Apple or Google, in which case we receive your email address from that provider. A unique user ID is generated and associated with your data. VisaStay has its own separate account, even if you use the same email address in our other apps.

Profile information

Your passport nationality and home currency, used to work out visa rules and show amounts. Never sold or shared with advertisers.

Trips and visas

Trips you create: destination country, city, start and end dates, the passport used for entry, visa type or program, and optional notes. Visas you save in My Visas: visa type, country, and dates. Destinations you star.

Notifications and emails

If you turn on push or email notifications, we store your alert rules (for example, days before a visa expires), whether you want the monthly digest, and your device's push token.

Additional alert recipients

If you choose to send alert emails to someone else (for example a partner, accountant, or employer), we store the email address you enter and send that person only the alerts you set up. They are not signed up for anything else, and you can remove them at any time in the app. Please only add people who are happy to receive these emails.

Visa info reports

If you use "Report a visa info change", we receive what you write, the country it concerns, and your account email so we can follow up.

Location and photos

VisaStay does not use your location. Shareable trip cards use a photo you pick for that card only; the app never gets access to your photo library and the photo is not uploaded.

Biometric lock (optional)

If you turn on biometric app lock, authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system (Face ID or fingerprint). We never receive, transmit, or store any biometric data — the app only stores a yes/no setting on your device.

Device and usage data

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking data, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat managing your Pro status. We receive only whether Pro is active. Your payment card details are never sent to or stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate visa days, Schengen 90/180 windows, and tax residency day counts
  • To send the push notifications and emails you set up (visa expiry, passport expiry, tax residency, Schengen alerts, monthly digest), to you and to anyone you add
  • To send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset
  • To review visa info reports you send
  • To keep track of whether Pro is active on your account
  • To let you export or delete your data
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

3. Who Can See Your Data

Your trips and visas are visible only to your account. Alert emails go only to you and to the recipients you add.

5. Data Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all personal data associated with it is permanently deleted from our systems within 7 days, except where retention is required by applicable law. Anonymized, non-identifiable aggregates may be kept indefinitely.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability — export your data in a machine-readable format (in the app: Profile → Export My Data)
  • Restriction / objection — ask us to limit or stop processing your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (for example, email notifications), turn it off at any time in the app

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@headfirstservices.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Deleting Your Account and Data

Delete your account in the app at Profile → Delete Account. If you can't use the app, email privacy@headfirstservices.com from the address on your account and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

Deleting your VisaStay account does not affect accounts in our other apps — each app keeps its own separate data. Full instructions: Delete your VisaStay account.

8. Data Breach Notification

If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights and freedoms at risk, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where the law requires, notify affected users without undue delay by email at the address on their account, and describe what happened, the data affected, the likely consequences, and what we are doing about it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, this is a transfer of personal data outside your region, covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated into our agreements with our processors.

10. Children's Privacy

VisaStay is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact privacy@headfirstservices.com and we will delete it promptly.

11. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest, row-level security on every database table so you can only access your own data, and bcrypt password hashing. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.

12. Changes to This Policy

When we update this policy we will change the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, we will notify you by email or in-app notice at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Continuing to use VisaStay after that date means you accept the updated policy.
