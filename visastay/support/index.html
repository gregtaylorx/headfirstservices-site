VisaStay support

Help with VisaStay, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Email support@headfirstservices.com. Tell us what happened, what phone you're using, and what you expected instead. We usually reply within two business days.

Privacy questions: privacy@headfirstservices.com.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you. It opens straight in the app.

I bought Pro but the app shows Free

Go to Profile → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by your app store: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. The Founder Lifetime Pass never renews.

A visa rule looks wrong

Use Report a visa info change in the app. Every report is checked against official sources. See our sources.

I'm not getting alerts

Check that notifications are allowed for VisaStay in your phone's settings, and that the alert is turned on in the app. Email alerts and extra recipients need Pro.

Get a copy of my data

Go to Profile → Export My Data. Pro also has CSV and PDF export of your trips.
