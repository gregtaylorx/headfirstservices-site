Delete your VisaStay account

VisaStay has its own account, separate from our other apps. Deleting it never affects your accounts in other HeadFirst Services apps.

Fastest: delete in the app

  1. Open VisaStay and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This deletes your account and sign-in, your profile (email, passport nationality, home currency), all trips, saved visas, starred destinations, alert recipients, and notification settings.

Can't use the app? Email us

Email privacy@headfirstservices.com from the address on your account (or tell us that address). We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days and confirm by email.

Request account deletion

Before you delete

  • Want a copy first? Use Profile → Export My Data in the app.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an app store subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account so you aren't charged again.
