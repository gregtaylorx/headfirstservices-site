How it works

A gentle habit, built to become unnecessary

Practice in short rounds at one meal a day. When your child has the habit, ChewPaca suggests you stop using the app. That's the point.

🦙

Chews along

When your child chews with their lips together, their animal chews along and hoofprints appear one by one. It's a quiet, happy marker of steady progress.

🗣️

You stay the coach

After each short round the app gives you one specific thing to say. That sentence is what teaches the habit. The animal just holds their attention while you say it.

🎁

Real rewards they choose

Good rounds earn tokens. A few tokens swap for a small reward you agreed on together, like choosing the bedtime story or the music in the car.

🐴

A new animal for every reward

Each reward delivered sends the animal to the barn, and your child names and styles the next one. The herd grows with every reward.

📈

Four stages to independence

Learning, Practicing, Maintaining and Fading out. Rounds get a little longer and rewards take a few more tokens as the habit forms.

👨‍👩‍👧

Made for families

As many children as you like, an optional grown-up PIN, and a backup file you control for moving to a new phone.

Privacy

We never see your child

The camera works entirely on your device. Video is looked at and immediately discarded — never recorded, never saved, never uploaded. There's no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no server of ours for anything to be sent to.
