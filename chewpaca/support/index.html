ChewPaca support

Help with ChewPaca, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Email ChewPaca@HeadFirstServices.com and tell us what happened, what phone you're using, and what you expected instead. Please don't include your child's full name, photos, videos, or health details — we don't need them to help. Privacy questions: privacy@headfirstservices.com.

Quick fixes

The camera cannot see a face

Move the phone closer (about an arm's length), add light, and angle it slightly upward toward your child's face. Make sure camera permission is allowed for ChewPaca in your phone's settings.

I bought the unlock but it's locked again

Open Settings → Purchases and restore, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

Moving to a new phone

Export your child's progress in Settings, import it on the new phone, then restore your unlock in Settings → Purchases.

When to stop and ask someone

Some children physically cannot chew with their mouth closed. Blocked nasal breathing, enlarged tonsils or adenoids, allergies, a deviated septum, or oral-motor differences can all make it genuinely hard or uncomfortable. Practicing will not fix any of those.

If your child has pain while chewing, coughs or chokes during meals, struggles with certain textures, or usually breathes through their mouth, mention what you have noticed to your pediatrician, dentist, or an ENT before continuing. If there is little change after a couple of weeks of regular practice, ChewPaca shows this list again as a reminder before the next session.

ChewPaca is not a medical, dental, feeding, or swallowing assessment. Stop and seek advice from an appropriate clinician if you have concerns.

Why we don't say "nothing ever leaves your phone"

It would be a better line, and plenty of apps use it. We think it would be slightly untrue, so here is the precise version. Nothing goes to us — there is no ChewPaca server, no account system, no analytics, and no way to receive your child's name, their progress, or a single frame of video. But your phone backs up its apps: unless you have turned that off, iOS copies app data to your iCloud and Android copies it to your Google account — ChewPaca included. That backup holds your child's name or nickname, an age range, their practice history and badges, in your account, under your password. It never contains video or audio. To exclude it, see the Privacy Policy.
