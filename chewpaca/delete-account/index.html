Your ChewPaca data

ChewPaca has no account, so there is nothing to delete on our side. We never receive your child's name, progress, or any video — there is no ChewPaca server.

Delete ChewPaca's data from your phone

  • Remove everything: uninstall ChewPaca. All of its data on the phone is erased.
  • Phone backups: your iCloud or Google backup may hold a copy of the app's data. To remove it, see Phone backups in the Privacy Policy.
