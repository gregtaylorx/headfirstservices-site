Short-stay / tourist entry rules.

What this checker shows

For each passport and destination, VisaStay holds the standard short-stay entry rule: whether you can enter visa-free, get a visa on arrival, apply online (eVisa or electronic travel authorisation), or need a visa before you travel — plus the maximum stay per entry, whether it can be extended in-country, and any pre-arrival requirements.

It covers tourist-style short stays only. Work, study, residence and digital nomad visas have their own rules and durations; check those with the destination's official immigration authority. If you hold one of those visas, the VisaStay app lets you track your stay using your own visa's dates.

Why use the app instead of a spreadsheet?

One lookup tells you today's rule. The app keeps counting: it tracks every trip, totals your Schengen days across the rolling 180-day window, flags future trips that would break a limit before you book, and reminds you before your stay ends.

Planning information only — not legal or immigration advice. Entry rules change, sometimes without notice. Always confirm with the destination's official immigration authority or embassy before you travel, especially for long stays, unusual passports, or past overstays.
