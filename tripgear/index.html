Pack less. Forget nothing.

TripGear builds your packing list from where you're going, the real weather forecast, how you're traveling and what you'll be doing — then gets smarter every time you travel.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play
What's coming

Built for carry-on travelers

Digital nomads, frequent flyers and one-bag minimalists: TripGear plans a lean capsule wardrobe instead of a generic checklist.

🌦️

Weather-aware

Live 16-day forecasts, or last year's weather for trips further out. Rain, cold, heat and snow days are flagged.

🗺️

Multi-destination trips

Add every leg. Each gets its own weather, and the list merges intelligently.

🎒

Transport and bag aware

Plane, train or car; carry-on, backpack or checked. Liquids and restricted items flagged for flights.

👕

Realistic quantities

Shirts, underwear and socks sized to your trip length and laundry access, so you don't haul two weeks of clothes for a week away.

✅

Pack mode + outfits

Check items off as you pack, track bag weight against your limit, and plan which outfit you'll wear each day.

🧠

Learns from every trip

A 30-second debrief records what you used, never touched, or wished you'd brought. Unused items drop off and forgotten ones get added next time.
