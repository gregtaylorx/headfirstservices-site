TripGear frequently asked questions

TripGear

What is TripGear?

A packing list app that builds your list from where you're going, the weather forecast, how you're traveling, and what you'll be doing — then learns from what you actually used after each trip.

When is it available?

TripGear is in testing now and is coming to both the App Store and Google Play.

Does it work for trips with several stops?

Yes. Add every leg; each destination gets its own weather, and the list merges them.

How far ahead can it check the weather?

Live forecasts cover about 16 days ahead. For trips further out, TripGear uses last year's weather for the same dates.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Saved lists stay available without a signal.

Pro and your data

Is TripGear free?

Yes — you can build complete lists for free. Pro adds unlimited lists, templates and bag profiles, laundry and re-wear planning, shared lists, and learning from your post-trip debriefs.

What is a debrief?

After a trip, a 30-second check-in records what you used, never touched, or wished you'd brought. Pro uses it to drop unused items and add forgotten ones next time.

Does TripGear use my location?

No. It uses only the destinations you enter, to fetch their weather.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account in the app. Full details: Delete your TripGear account.
