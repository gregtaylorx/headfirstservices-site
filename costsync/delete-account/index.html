Delete your CostSync account

Draft. CostSync has not been released yet. This page will be finalized before launch.

CostSync has its own account, separate from our other apps. Deleting it never affects your accounts in other HeadFirst Services apps.

Fastest: delete in the app

  1. Open CostSync and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This deletes your account and sign-in, all trips, expenses, receipts, budgets, and wallets.

Can't use the app? Email us

Email privacy@headfirstservices.com from the address on your account (or tell us that address). We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days and confirm by email.

Request account deletion

Before you delete

  • Want a copy first? Use Profile → Export My Data in the app.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an app store subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account so you aren't charged again.

More detail: CostSync Privacy Policy.
