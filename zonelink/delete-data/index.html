Delete your ZoneLink data

You can delete some of your ZoneLink data without deleting your account, or delete everything at once.

Favorites and the zones in them

To remove a zone, tap the trash icon next to it. To remove a whole Favorite, open its settings (the gear icon in History) and tap Delete favorite. Either one disappears from the app immediately.

Retention: so your devices stay in sync, a deleted Favorite or zone is marked as deleted on our servers rather than erased right away. It is never shown, returned to the app, or used for anything after you delete it. Deleting your account (below) erases it permanently and immediately.

Jet lag tracking

Go to Profile → Travel and turn off jet lag tracking to stop ZoneLink asking when your device's time zone changes. ZoneLink never uses your location for this. Any jet lag details already saved are erased when you delete your account.

Get a copy first

Go to Profile → Export My Data to download everything ZoneLink holds about you.

Everything (full account deletion)

To permanently delete your ZoneLink account and all of its data, immediately and with nothing retained:

Delete my account
