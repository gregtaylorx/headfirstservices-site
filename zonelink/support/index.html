ZoneLink support

Help with ZoneLink, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Email support@headfirstservices.com. Tell us what happened, what phone you're using, and what you expected instead. We usually reply within two business days.

Privacy questions: privacy@headfirstservices.com.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.

I bought Pro but the app shows Free

Go to Profile → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

My widget stopped updating

Open ZoneLink and view the Favorite the widget shows — that refreshes the widget's data.

A time or DST change looks wrong

Email us the zone and the date. ZoneLink follows the official IANA time zone data, so we can check whether a rule changed.

Get a copy of my data

Go to Profile → Export My Data.
