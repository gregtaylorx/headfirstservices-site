Features

Find a time that works for everyone

Built for remote teams, freelancers with international clients, long-distance relationships, and frequent travelers.

🎚️

Scrubbable time-zone ribbon

Drag across the day and every zone moves with you, banded by sleep, work, and awake-but-off hours.

🏆

Ranked best times to meet

ZoneLink scans ahead and lists the windows where everyone is in working hours — no scrubbing required.

📤

Shareable overlap card

Send one image showing the best time in each person's own local time, so the invite does the explaining for you. Free for everyone.

⏰

Automatic DST alerts

Get a heads-up in the two weeks before any of your zones changes its clocks, before a recurring call quietly shifts by an hour.

✈️

Jet lag tracking

Your own zone follows your travels — auto-detected or set manually, with no location data used.

⭐

Favorites

Save every group you coordinate with — teams, clients, family — color-coded and renamed your way.

📱

Home-screen widget

Zones Clock shows live local time for every zone on your first Favorite, on iPhone and Android, without opening the app.

🔒

Biometric app lock

Fingerprint or Face ID keeps your boards private, and it works offline.

🚫

No ads, ever

No ads in the free tier or in Pro. The free features stay free.
