ZoneLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HeadFirstServices LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles your information in ZoneLink — a visual, sleep-aware time-zone planner. It covers ZoneLink only; each of our apps has its own policy and its own separate database.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account you provide your email address and a password (stored as a salted hash — we never see it in plain text), or you sign in with Apple or Google, in which case we receive your email address from that provider. A unique user ID is generated and associated with your data. ZoneLink has its own separate account, even if you use the same email address in our other apps.

Favorites and zones

Favorites you save (name and color) and the entries in each one: a label you choose — such as a person's name, team, or city — plus a time zone and optional work and sleep hours. The zones on your Home workbench.

If you add another person (for example, a colleague's name and time zone), you are giving us limited information about them on your behalf. Only the label and time zone you enter are stored; we do not verify or enrich it, and only your account can see it.

Jet lag tracking

If you use jet lag tracking, we store the time zones you traveled from and to, your arrival time, and your device's last time zone. ZoneLink reads your phone's time zone setting only — it never uses your location or GPS. You can turn tracking off in Profile → Travel.

Biometric lock (optional)

If you turn on biometric app lock, authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system (Face ID or fingerprint). We never receive, transmit, or store any biometric data — the app only stores a yes/no setting on your device.

Device and usage data

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking data, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat managing your Pro status. We receive only whether Pro is active. Your payment card details are never sent to or stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To show your zones, rank the best times to meet, and warn you about daylight saving changes
  • To sync your Favorites across your devices
  • To send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset
  • To keep track of whether Pro is active on your account
  • To let you export or delete your data
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

3. Who Can See Your Data

Your Favorites and zones are visible only to your account. When you share a best-time card, you choose where it goes; the card is an image created on your phone.

5. Data Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all personal data associated with it is permanently deleted from our systems within 7 days, except where retention is required by applicable law. Anonymized, non-identifiable aggregates may be kept indefinitely.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability — export your data in a machine-readable format (in the app: Profile → Export My Data)
  • Restriction / objection — ask us to limit or stop processing your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (for example, email notifications), turn it off at any time in the app

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@headfirstservices.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Deleting Your Account and Data

Delete your account in the app at Profile → Delete Account. It is deleted immediately. If you can't use the app, email privacy@headfirstservices.com and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

You can also delete individual Favorites or zones without deleting your account. They disappear from the app immediately, but for sync reliability the record is kept on our servers, marked as deleted, and never shown or used again. Deleting your account removes everything permanently. See Delete ZoneLink data.

Deleting your ZoneLink account does not affect accounts in our other apps — each app keeps its own separate data. Full instructions: Delete your ZoneLink account.

8. Data Breach Notification

If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights and freedoms at risk, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where the law requires, notify affected users without undue delay by email at the address on their account, and describe what happened, the data affected, the likely consequences, and what we are doing about it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, this is a transfer of personal data outside your region, covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated into our agreements with our processors.

10. Children's Privacy

ZoneLink is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact privacy@headfirstservices.com and we will delete it promptly.

11. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest, row-level security on every database table so you can only access your own data, and bcrypt password hashing. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.

12. Changes to This Policy

When we update this policy we will change the "Last updated" date above. For material changes, we will notify you by email or in-app notice at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Continuing to use ZoneLink after that date means you accept the updated policy.
