ZoneLink frequently asked questions

About ZoneLink

What is ZoneLink?

ZoneLink is a time-zone overlap planner. Add the people or places you coordinate with, and the Slider shows every zone's local time side by side on one scrubbable ribbon — so you can see, at a glance, who's asleep, working, or awake at any moment, and drag to any point in the day to check a specific time.

Who is ZoneLink for?

Anyone juggling people or plans across time zones: remote teams, freelancers with international clients, long-distance relationships, and frequent travelers who want to know what time it is back home — or at their next destination — without doing the math themselves.

How is this different from a plain world clock app?

A world clock shows the current time per city. ZoneLink's Slider shows an entire day at once, color-banded by sleep/work/awake hours for every zone, and lets you scrub to any hour — today or a future date — to instantly see whether a time works for everyone. It also groups zones into named Favorites you can save and reopen.

What is a Favorite?

A Favorite is a saved group of zones — e.g. "Design Team" (you, a designer in London, a PM in Singapore) or "Family" (you, your parents back home). Save a Favorite from Home and it appears in History; tap it any time to reopen the Slider for that exact group.

What's the difference between Home and History?

Home is the workbench — pick zones, see the Slider, optionally star and save. History is where every saved Favorite lives, starred ones pinned to the top. Long-press any History Favorite and choose "Copy to Home" to make it your live, editable Home screen — edits to either stay in sync.

Using the Slider

How do I check a specific time, like 3pm my time?

Drag anywhere on the ribbon to scrub to that moment — every zone's local time updates live as you drag. Drag near either edge and it keeps scrolling on its own, faster the closer you get to the edge, so you can reach hours off-screen without repeated swipes.

What do the colors on the ribbon mean?

Each zone's row is banded by local time of day — typically sleep, work, and awake-but-off-hours — so you can tell at a glance whether a proposed time lands during someone's workday or the middle of their night.

What do the 12h / 24h / 2d / 4d controls do?

They zoom the ribbon's visible time window — 12 or 24 hours for a single day's detail, or 2d/4d to see multiple days at once for planning further ahead.

How many zones can I add?

Free accounts can add up to 5 zones on the Home workbench. ZoneLink Pro removes that cap — add as many as you need.

Does ZoneLink handle Daylight Saving Time automatically?

Yes — zones are IANA time-zone identifiers, so DST transitions are handled automatically. ZoneLink also shows a banner in the two weeks before any zone on your Favorite changes its clocks, so a time that works today doesn't quietly stop working after a DST shift.

Best Times to Meet

What is "Best times to meet"?

A ranked list, shown right under the Slider, of the closest upcoming windows where the most people in your Favorite are awake and working — so you don't have to scrub the ribbon yourself to find one. Windows outside everyone's regular work hours are still shown (as long as nobody's asleep) but flagged with a clock icon.

Can I share a good time with the people I'm coordinating with?

Yes — tap Share on the best-times list to generate a branded image card showing the top window in each person's own local time, ready to drop into a text or email. This is free on every account, not a Pro feature.

Jet Lag Tracking

What is jet-lag tracking?

A small indicator on your own ("Me") zone showing roughly how many days you have left to adjust after a time-zone change — based on the zone you just left and the zone you're in now. It's free on every account.

How does ZoneLink know I landed somewhere new?

It compares your phone's time zone setting each time you open the app to what it saw last time — no location or GPS data is used, just the zone your device already reports. Turn this off any time in Profile → Travel.

Can I set it manually?

Yes — Profile → "Set jet lag manually", or tap the Jet lag badge on your Me row, works whether or not auto-detection is turned on.

Home-Screen Widgets

Is there a home-screen widget?

Yes, on both Android and iOS — the Zones Clock widget shows live local time for every zone on a Favorite, right on your home screen, no need to open the app. It's included free, no Pro purchase required. On Android: long-press your home screen → Widgets → ZoneLink to add it, then use its gear icon to choose which Favorite it displays. On iOS: touch and hold your home screen → tap + in the corner → search ZoneLink → add it; the iOS widget currently shows your first Favorite (per-widget board picking is coming to iOS).

Why did my widget stop updating?

Reopen the app and view the Favorite the widget is bound to — that republishes fresh data to the widget.

Subscription & Pricing

Is ZoneLink free?

Yes, permanently — the Slider, up to 5 zones, DST alerts, the ranked best-times list, the shareable overlap card, and one home-screen widget are all free forever, no trial required and no ads. New accounts also get 14 days of full Pro access (plus a 2-day grace period) to try everything unlimited before deciding.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your account settles onto the permanent Free plan — nothing locks up. Zones on Home cap at 5, and saving a named Favorite to History requires Pro; everything you saved before the trial ended keeps working. Upgrade any time from the paywall's Restore/Upgrade options.

What does ZoneLink Pro add?

Unlimited zones on Home and unlimited saved Favorites in History, unlocked with a single one-time purchase.

Is ZoneLink Pro a subscription?

No — ZoneLink Pro is a single one-time (lifetime) purchase, with no recurring charge.

How do I restore my purchase after reinstalling or switching devices?

Go to Profile → Restore Purchases, or use the Restore link on the upgrade screen. This reconnects your existing Pro purchase to your account.

Privacy & Data

What data does ZoneLink store?

Your email address, the Favorites/zones you create (names, time zones, colors), and — if you use jet-lag tracking — the two time zones and date involved. No location data is collected; jet-lag tracking reads your device's time zone setting only, never GPS.

Where is my data stored?

Securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted on AWS infrastructure. Favorites also sync to your device for offline use and stay in sync once you're back online.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Go to Profile → Delete Account, or email privacy@headfirstservices.com directly. We'll delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

Does ZoneLink share data with VisaStay or other Outbound Suite apps?

No — each app in the Outbound Suite has its own separate account system and database. Nothing is shared between ZoneLink and VisaStay unless a future cross-app feature explicitly asks your permission first.
