About

How we build

HeadFirst Services is a mobile app development studio, not just a portfolio of side projects. Every app is engineered on the same foundation and built to a real standard: production Flutter apps, live on the App Store and Play Store, backed by proper architecture.

⚙️

Production engineering, done properly

Our apps share a common technical foundation — Flutter/Dart, Supabase (Postgres + Auth), Riverpod, row-level security on every table — the same stack used to ship real apps to production, not prototypes.

🎯

One job, done well

Every app in the suite is built around a single, specific problem — not a bundle of half-finished features.

🔒

Privacy by default

Each app owns its own isolated backend and its own account system — or, like ChewPaca, has no backend at all and runs entirely on your phone. Deleting one app's account never touches another, and cross-app data sharing is always explicit and user-consented.

📈

Built to grow

The suite is designed to keep expanding — more apps for frequent travelers, and eventually product lines entirely outside this theme.
